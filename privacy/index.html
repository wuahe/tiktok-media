Privacy Policy

Hermes Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner to post the owner's own content to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data accessed: the OAuth access token and basic profile info (open_id, display name) of the owner's account, used solely to call the TikTok Content Posting API. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.

No data from other TikTok users is collected. Images posted are hosted publicly on this site only for TikTok to fetch them.

To revoke access, remove the app in TikTok Settings > Security > Apps and services.
